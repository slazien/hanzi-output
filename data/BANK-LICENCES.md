# Licences for data/bank.json

The sentence bank combines material under different licences. Each sentence records its source.

| Source | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [HSK Sentences Audio](https://huggingface.co/datasets/no7z/hsk-sentences-audio) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Sentences, translations, word splitting, pinyin and grammar points |
| [Chinese Grammar Wiki](https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/) © AllSet Learning, via the [UD Chinese-Beginner](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Chinese-Beginner) treebank | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 | **Non-commercial use only.** Sentences, translations, pinyin and pattern names |
| [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) | CC BY 2.0 FR | Each sentence keeps its Tatoeba number; authorship is shown at `https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<number>`. Sentences were converted to simplified characters; word splitting and pinyin were added |
| Sentences in `tools/own_sentences.tsv` | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Written for this app |
| [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Word meanings in the bank's dictionary |
| [complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary) by Yanis Zafirópulos | MIT | Word list and HSK levels used while building |

Because the bank includes Chinese Grammar Wiki content, the bank as a whole may only be used non-commercially.
