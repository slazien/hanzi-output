Juzi 句子: Chinese sentence practice

Juzi is a free, non-commercial web app for learners of Mandarin Chinese. You read an English sentence, write it in simplified Chinese, and get your answer graded and explained.

Open the app

What it does

  • Whole-sentence practice. Sentences come from a stored bank of about 16,000, chosen to fit the characters and words you already know.
  • Feedback on your answer. An AI model scores your translation, shows a corrected version and a reference answer with pinyin, and answers follow-up questions. It uses your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key.
  • Spaced review. You rate each sentence, and it comes back when you are likely to be about to forget it.
  • Weak areas and progress. The app shows which themes, grammar patterns and kinds of mistake need work, and how your results change over time.

Accounts and Google sign-in

The app works without an account, keeping your progress in your browser. Signing in is optional. If you choose Continue with Google, Juzi uses your Google account only to identify you, so that your practice progress can be saved to your account and kept in step across your devices.

Juzi asks Google for your basic profile: your email address, name and profile picture. It does not ask for, and cannot read, your contacts, email, files or anything else in your Google account. See the privacy policy for details.

Who makes it

Juzi is a personal project maintained by the GitHub user slazien. The source code is public at github.com/slazien/hanzi-output. Questions and requests can be raised at github.com/slazien/hanzi-output/issues.

Sentence sources and their licences are listed in the app under Settings.
